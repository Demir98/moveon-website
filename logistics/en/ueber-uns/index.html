About Move On

A young company with a clear mission: reliable last-mile delivery – rooted in the Wetterau–Taunus region, with reach across Germany and Europe.

Founded on 1 March 2025

Move On GmbH was founded on 1 March 2025 in Altenstadt, with the goal of organizing last-mile delivery professionally and predictably. From our depot in Altenstadt, we cover the area that stretches from Wetterau to Taunus, and handle transport and delivery jobs across Germany and Europe beyond it.

In a short time, that idea has grown into a company with a close-knit team and fixed route structures – rooted in the region and just as ready to deliver reliably well beyond it.

Our role in the CEP network

Move On does not operate as its own parcel brand, but as a transport partner in the network of various CEP providers (courier, express and parcel services). We handle the operational last-mile delivery: shipments arrive at our depot in Altenstadt and are distributed from there onto fixed routes.

This partner model lets us focus entirely on what we do best – reliable, locally-rooted delivery – while the parcel services focus on sorting and network management.

How we see ourselves

  • Reliability: fixed routes, dedicated points of contact.
  • Punctuality: predictable delivery times for recipients and partners.
  • Professionalism: trained drivers, careful handling of every shipment.
  • Regional roots, national reach: local knowledge of Wetterau and Taunus, with coverage across Germany and Europe.
