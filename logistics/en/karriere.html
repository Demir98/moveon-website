Open position at the Altenstadt depot

We're hiring a driver (m/f/d)

Join a team of around 50 drivers who handle last-mile delivery in the Wetterau–Taunus region on around 50 routes every day – with jobs that can take you nationwide and internationally too.

What we offer

  • Fixed routes from the Altenstadt depot
  • A close-knit, collegial team
  • Thorough onboarding
  • Predictable working hours

What we expect from you

  • Class B driving licence
  • Reliability and punctuality
  • Physical fitness for loading and unloading
  • Friendly manner with customers
